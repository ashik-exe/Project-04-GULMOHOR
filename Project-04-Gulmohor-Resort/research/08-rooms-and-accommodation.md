## 2. In-Depth Room Specifications

### A. Couple Deluxe Room (Corroborated via Room #2 Walkthrough)
- **Room Configuration:** Spacious bedroom area designed for two guests.
- **Bedding:** Queen-size bed with crisp white bedsheets, headboard, quilt/blanket, and dual side tables.
- **Seating & Furniture:** 2-seater upholstered sofa, coffee table, writing desk, chair, wardrobe/almirah with coat hangers.
- **In-Room Electronics:** Split-system Air Conditioner, flat-screen wall-mounted LED TV, electric water kettle for hot water and tea, compact personal mini-refrigerator.
- **Complimentary Inclusions:** Bottled mineral water, complimentary tea packets, sugar, cups.
- **Attached Washroom:** Ceramic tile finishes, modern commode, ceramic washbasin with large mirror, toiletries/soap, clean bath towels, and an architecturally separated shower enclosure.
- **Outdoor View:** Glass sliding door / window leading to a wide common terrace balcony overlooking the swimming pool, garden lounge, and distant rural landscape.

### B. Double Deluxe Room
- **Room Configuration:** Expanded family/group room designed to comfortably sleep 4 adults.
- **Bedding:** Two double beds.
- **Furnishings:** Air conditioning, attached private washroom, television, wardrobe, and balcony access.
- **Value Proposition:** Ideal for nuclear families with children or small groups of friends traveling together.

### C. Premium Suite Room
- **Room Configuration:** Luxury multi-zone suite consisting of a separate living/reception lounge and a private master bedroom.
- **Lounge Amenities:** Luxury sofa set, center table, dining/work area.
- **Bedroom Amenities:** King-size bed, luxury wooden wardrobe, dresser with illuminated mirror, flat-screen TV.
- **Washroom:** Premium sanitary fittings, high-spec shower cabin with glass partition, premium toiletries.
- **Tariff:** BDT 12,000 per night (includes complimentary breakfast).

## 3. Stay Policies & Check-in Details

- **Complimentary Breakfast:** Included in all standard overnight room bookings (BDT 7,000 / 9,000 / 12,000 tiers). Breakfast is served from 8:30 AM to 10:30 AM in the ground-floor restaurant.
- **Breakfast Menu:** Traditional hot Bengali breakfast: freshly made Parathas, spicy Daal, fried/boiled Egg, mixed Vegetable bhaji, and Milk/Black Tea.
- **Day Stay Use:** Rooms can be rented for daytime relaxation (without overnight stay) under the Day-Long Package (BDT 6,500 with meals, or BDT 5,000 room-only).
- **Couple Accommodation Policy:** Standard hospitality verification applies; couples traveling together are advised to carry identification.
- **Check-in / Check-out Times:** Standard regional hospitality hours typically observe 12:00 PM check-in and 11:00 AM check-out (exact grace period rules flagged for client confirmation in Q01).
