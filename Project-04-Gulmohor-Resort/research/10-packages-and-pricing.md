## 2. Package Breakdown & Comparative Value

### A. The BDT 6,500 Day-Long Leisure Package
This is the resort's most popular retail leisure product for city residents:
- **Typical Hours:** 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
- **Target Market:** Couples and professionals seeking a refreshing weekend break without staying overnight.
- **Economic Value:** For BDT 6,500, a couple receives a private air-conditioned Deluxe room for freshening up and resting, two complete multi-course lunches, afternoon tea and snacks, and unrestricted access to the swimming pool and gardens.

### B. Overnight Stays (BDT 7,000 – 12,000)
- Offers strong competitive value compared to neighboring Mawa expressway luxury resorts that often charge BDT 10,000–18,000+ per night.
- Includes complimentary breakfast for the full room capacity.

## 3. Commercial Terms & Event Booking Conditions

- **Advance Deposit:** Advance payment (typically 30% to 50% via bKash, Nagad, or bank transfer) is required to secure room holds and exclusive event lawn reservations.
- **Event Lead Time:** Wedding and reunion reservations must be booked at least 15–30 days in advance to allow time for lawn decoration, stage construction, and catering procurement.
