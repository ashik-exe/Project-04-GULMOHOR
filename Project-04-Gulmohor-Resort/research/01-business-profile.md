## 4. Target Customer Segments

1. **Urban Day-Trippers & Couples (Dhaka Metro):** Couples seeking private, secure daytime leisure, swimming pool access, and peaceful dining away from metropolitan crowds.
2. **Families & Children:** Multigenerational families visiting on weekends for picnics, child-friendly recreation at the Kids Zone, and safe swimming.
3. **Destination Wedding & Gaye Holud Parties:** High-budget social celebrations seeking an all-in-one scenic outdoor venue for bridal photography, outdoor marquee stages, and indoor dining.
4. **Corporate Retreats & Alumni Reunions:** Corporate teams, university batches, and school alumni groups (e.g. 50–150 attendees) requiring buffet dining, music setups, and lawn seating.
