## 3. Communication & Booking Workflow via Facebook

- **Primary Conversion Action:** The "Book Now" button and post captions direct visitors to dial  or open a WhatsApp conversation.
- **Messenger Usage:** Automated greetings and inquiries are accepted via Messenger, but staff routinely redirect guests to WhatsApp for sending booking advances and receiving location pins.
- **Deficiencies Identified:**
  - Lack of pinned pricing posts: Visitors frequently have to comment *"price please?"* or *"package cost?"* on posts.
  - Absence of a real-time reservation calendar: Inability to check whether rooms or the event lawn are available on specific upcoming dates.
