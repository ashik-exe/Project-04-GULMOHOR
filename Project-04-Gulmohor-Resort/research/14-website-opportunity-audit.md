## 1. Current Digital State vs. Ideal State

| Dimension | Current State (Facebook & WhatsApp Only) | Recommended Future State (With Dedicated Website) |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary Domain** | None (Facebook URL only) | Dedicated branded domain (`gulmohorresort.com` / `.com.bd`) |
| **Search Engine Presence** | Confused with Indian resort (`gulmohor.in`) | Dominant Google Knowledge Panel & local SEO authority |
| **Room Inventory Presentation** | Hidden inside 13-minute video vlogs | Clean interactive cards with high-res photos & specs |
| **Pricing Transparency** | Fragmented across captions and chats | Authoritative, itemized tariff cards with package toggle |
| **Booking Mechanism** | Manual, unassisted WhatsApp chatting | Seamless booking modal with automated WhatsApp routing |
| **Event Inquiries** | Phone call or Messenger ping | Tailored event submission form capturing dates & pax |
| **Visual Showcase** | Compressed Facebook reels | Full-screen hero video, 1080p galleries, lightbox viewer |

## 2. Competitive Advantage in the Regional Market

Munshiganj and the Mawa Expressway corridor have rapidly evolved into Dhaka's premier resort hub, competing with:
- **Dhali's Amber Nivaas:** Large resort, high capacity, traditional rural village style.
- **Modern Park & Resort:** Theme park and amusement style.
- **The Slow Deck / The Slow Garden:** Riverfront boutique aesthetics.

### Gulmohor's Winning Differentiation
Gulmohor is distinctly positioned as **The Neoclassical Boutique Villa**:
- Not an overcrowded amusement park, and not a rustic thatch hut.
- An elegant white colonial-style mansion with European-style Roman columns, black wrought-iron balconies, private rose gardens, and a crystal-clear 70x35 ft pool.
- Slogan *"You Find Peace"* and *"Find A Home Close To Home"* captures an upscale staycation demographic.

A website designed around this neoclassical luxury aesthetic will give Gulmohor Resort a significant marketing advantage.
