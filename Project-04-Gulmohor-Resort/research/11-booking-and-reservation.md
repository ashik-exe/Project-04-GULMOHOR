## 1. Current Step-by-Step Guest Booking Journey

Because Gulmohor Resort lacks an official website or automated booking engine, the current reservation workflow is entirely manual:

```text
[Step 1: Discovery]
Guest discovers resort via Facebook Post/Reel or YouTube Travel Vlog
       ↓
[Step 2: Contact Search]
Guest locates telephone number (01911-498819) from Facebook header or vlog description
       ↓
[Step 3: Direct Inquiry]
Guest initiates phone call or WhatsApp message to inquire about date availability & room rates
       ↓
[Step 4: Manual Negotiation]
Resort staff manually checks handwritten ledger or mobile notes to verify room availability
Staff quotes package prices and sends bank/bKash payment details over WhatsApp chat
       ↓
[Step 5: Advance Payment]
Guest sends 30%-50% deposit via bKash / bank transfer and messages the transaction ID / screenshot
       ↓
[Step 6: Confirmation]
Staff acknowledges payment via WhatsApp chat and confirms the reservation
       ↓
[Step 7: Check-in & Final Settlement]
Guest arrives at resort, checks in, and settles the remaining balance in cash or mobile money
```

---

## 2. Identified Friction Points & Deficiencies

1. **High Inconvenience for Corporate & High-Net-Worth Clients:** Corporate bookers and international travelers expect instant self-service dates selection, credit card processing, and immediate email confirmation receipts.
2. **Delayed Response Times:** If phone lines are busy or staff are occupied on-site, inquiries go unanswered for hours, causing potential guests to book alternative resorts.
3. **No Centralized Date Availability Calendar:** Staff and customers cannot see which weekends or event lawns are blocked or open.
4. **Lack of Transparent Policy Disclosures:** Rules regarding child age limits, outside food, swimming costume requirements, and cancellation penalties are communicated verbally, leading to occasional disputes.

---

## 3. High-Impact Website Enhancements for Phase 02

To resolve these friction points, the future website demo must introduce:
1. **Direct WhatsApp Reservation Engine:** A clean, intuitive booking modal that collects Guest Name, Dates, Room Type, Guest Count, and automatically drafts an organized reservation message sent straight to WhatsApp `+8801911498819`.
2. **Transparent Rate Card & Package Toggle:** Clear side-by-side comparison of Day Packages vs. Overnight Stay rates with itemized inclusions.
3. **Dedicated Event Inquiry Form:** Tailored form for weddings, corporate picnics, and reunions collecting event type, expected headcount, and specific catering preferences.
4. **Interactive Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ):** An accordion addressing common pre-booking questions.
