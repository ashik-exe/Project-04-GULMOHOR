## 2. Facility Deep-Dive

### The Swimming Pool (Centerpiece Facility)
The swimming pool is the resort's primary visual attraction and revenue driver:
- **Contractor & Specifications:** Built by *QR Bangladesh Swimming Pool Store*, measuring **70 feet in length by 35 feet in width**, with a gradual depth profile suitable for adults and supervised children.
- **Water Quality:** Continuous circulation filtration system; turquoise tiled interior.
- **Night Features:** High-intensity underwater LED lights illuminate the water in vibrant cyan and blue hues.
- **Rules & Access:**
  - Day visitors: 400 BDT per hour per person.
  - Overnight staying guests: Complimentary access during designated hours.
  - Poolside loungers and shaded benches provided on paved surrounding patio.

### The Event Infrastructure
Gulmohor Resort offers a dual-venue capacity ideal for traditional Bangladeshi weddings:
1. **The Outdoor Lawn:** Used for pre-wedding rituals (Gaye Holud / Mehendi), musical evenings, and open-air receptions with draped canopies and fairy lights.
2. **The Indoor Hall Room:** Provides a climate-controlled banquet hall for formal wedding dinners, bride/groom stages, and corporate presentations.
