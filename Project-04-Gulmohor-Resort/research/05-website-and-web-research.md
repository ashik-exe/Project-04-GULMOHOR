# 05. Website & Web Audit — Gulmohor Resort

**Target Queries:** Gulmohor Resort Munshiganj, Gulmohor Resort Sreenagar, Gulmohor Resort Damla, gulmohorresort.com, gulmohorresortbd.com  
**Research Date:** November 2026  
**Evidence Linkage:** Source IDs S001, S003, S011, S012 | Fact IDs F026

---

## 1. Official Website Status: ABSENT

A comprehensive search of domain registries, Bangladesh tourism portals, hotel directories, and search indexes reveals:

> **Gulmohor Resort currently has NO official website.**

- There is no active custom domain registered or linked from any official channel.
- The business has not registered with major online travel agencies (OTAs) like Booking.com, Agoda, or MakeMyTrip (the MakeMyTrip and Agoda listings under "Gulmohar" represent Indian properties in Maharashtra and Jharkhand).
- Local booking directories (e.g., VromonGuide, BdResorts) mention the property in travel blogs and listicles of "Resorts near Dhaka / Mawa", but invariably cite the Facebook page and telephone number  for bookings.

## 3. Critical Business Consequences of Lacking a Website

1. **Lost Corporate Bookings:** Corporate HR managers and retreat planners require official websites with downloadable brochures, VAT/tax details, and formal inquiry submission forms.
2. **SEO Vulnerability:** Anyone searching *"Gulmohor Resort"* on Google is frequently exposed to the Indian resort (), causing brand confusion and bounce-offs.
3. **High Operational Friction:** Resort staff are bogged down answering repetitive questions about room tariffs, lunch menus, and check-in times on WhatsApp.
4. **Lack of Perceived Authority:** While competing resorts in Munshiganj (e.g., Dhali's Amber Nivaas) possess dedicated digital portals, Gulmohor's lack of a site diminishes its luxury positioning.
