## 1. Executive Overview

**Gulmohor Resort & Restaurant** (গুলমোহর রিসোর্ট) is an upscale boutique leisure retreat, dining establishment, and event destination situated in the serene village of Damla, within Sreenagar Upazila of Munshiganj District, Bangladesh. Located approximately 30 to 40 minutes south of Dhaka via the 8-lane Bangabandhu (Dhaka-Mawa) Expressway, the property caters to city dwellers seeking quick daytime escapes, family weekend stays, corporate retreats, and destination weddings.

The resort's centerpiece is a grand, two-story white neoclassical mansion featuring Romanesque/Corinthian columns, black ornamental wrought-iron terrace railings, and a massive 70 ft × 35 ft commercial swimming pool bordered by manicured lawns, blooming rose gardens, and a shaded outdoor lounge.

### Core Business Pillars
1. **Day Outings & Leisure:** High-volume daytime visitors attracted by the resort's swimming pool, lush gardens, children's play zone, and set-menu lunch packages.
2. **Overnight Accommodation:** Exclusive, well-appointed guest rooms including **Couple Deluxe Rooms**, **Double Deluxe Family Rooms**, and **Premium Suite Rooms** with attached modern washrooms and pool-facing balconies.
3. **Dining & Culinary Operations:** A full-service air-conditioned ground-floor restaurant overlooking the pool, an outdoor live BBQ/kebab kitchen, and catering buffet infrastructure serving traditional Bengali and Chinese cuisine.
4. **Events, Weddings & Reunions:** Highly popular venue for destination weddings, Gaye Holud ceremonies, school/university alumni reunions (e.g., DUFA 9596, Muslim High School 1985 Batch 40th anniversary), and corporate off-sites.

## 3. Digital Presence & Strategic Opportunity Assessment

Despite strong physical appeal, positive customer sentiment, and robust word-of-mouth visibility on YouTube and Facebook reels, Gulmohor Resort operates with a **critical digital gap**:

1. **Absence of an Official Website:** The business has no website domain (neither  nor ). Potential high-paying corporate clients, overseas non-resident Bangladeshis (NRBs) planning destination weddings, and travelers searching on Google cannot find official room inventories, verified pricing schedules, or reservation facilities.
2. **Discovery Friction:** Room rates and package details are scattered across video blogs and ephemeral Facebook post captions rather than accessible in a permanent, organized digital catalog.
3. **Reservation Bottlenecks:** Guests must call or message manually over WhatsApp. There is no automated booking request pipeline, reservation calendar, or inquiry capture mechanism.
4. **Massive Commercial Opportunity:** Developing a high-end, bespoke website demo will immediately elevate Gulmohor Resort above competing regional resorts (e.g., Dhali's Amber Nivaas, Modern Park, The Slow Deck), providing an authoritative platform for direct booking generation, event inquiries, and brand prestige.
