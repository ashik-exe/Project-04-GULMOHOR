# 09. Restaurant & Food Services — Gulmohor Resort

**Establishment:** Gulmohor Resort & Restaurant  
**Location:** Damla, Sreenagar, Munshiganj  
**Research Date:** November 2026  
**Evidence Linkage:** Fact IDs F002, F019, F020, F022 | Source IDs S001, S005, S006, S007

## 1. Dining Venues & Ambiance

Gulmohor operates a versatile culinary ecosystem catering to both intimate leisure diners and massive banquet parties:

1. **The Ground-Floor Indoor Restaurant:**
   - Fully air-conditioned dining hall situated on the ground floor of the main mansion.
   - Floor-to-ceiling glass windows offering clear views of the turquoise swimming pool, rose gardens, and outdoor landscape.
   - Clean, modern wooden tables with comfortable dining chairs.
2. **Poolside & Garden Terrace Seating:**
   - Open-air dining tables set up on the paved patio along the swimming pool edge and under the garden canopies.
   - Highly favored by evening guests enjoying cool breezes and illuminated pool reflections.
3. **The Live BBQ & Kebab Kitchen:**
   - Dedicated outdoor live cooking station (`লাইভ কিচেন`).
   - Equipped for live charcoal grilling of chicken boti kebab, shikh kebab, chicken chaap, and naan.
   - Promoted under the signature brand slogan: *"The Sun Goes Down. The Grill Heats Up. The Evening Comes Alive."*
4. **Banquet Catering & Buffet Infrastructure:**
   - Commercial kitchen and buffet service area equipped with stainless steel chafing dishes, soup warmers, and catering cutlery.
   - Capable of serving 100+ guests simultaneously for weddings, reunions, and corporate picnics.

## 2. Menu Profiles & Verified Dishes

### A. Complimentary Guest Breakfast Menu (Included in Overnight Stay)
- Freshly rolled, pan-fried Paratha (পরোটা)
- Traditional spiced Lentil Daal (ডাল)
- Egg (ডিম — Omelette / Poached / Boiled)
- Seasonal mixed Vegetable Bhaji (সবজি ভাজি)
- Hot Bengali Milk Tea / Black Tea (এক কাপ চা)

### B. Day-Long Package Lunch Menu (Included in BDT 6,500 Package)
- Chinese-style Egg/Vegetable Fried Rice (ফ্রাইড রাইস)
- Crispy Fried Chicken (চিকেন ফ্রাই)
- Spicy Chicken Chili Onion (চিকেন চিলি অনিয়ন)
- Fresh green garden salad

### C. Afternoon & Evening Snacks
- Crispy Chicken / Vegetable Patties (প্যাটিস)
- Traditional hot Piyaju / Fritters (পেঁয়াজু)
- Evening Tea and Coffee

### D. Live Barbecue & Evening Grill Specialties
- Chicken Tandoori & Tikka
- Live Charcoal Grilled Kebabs
- Butter Naan & Garlic Naan

### E. Special Occasion / Ramadan Iftar & Dinner Menu (Verified BDT 500 Special)
- Beverages: Fresh sweet yogurt Lacchi (লাচ্ছি), Cold Drinks
- Iftar Classics: Premium dates (খেজুর), spiced chickpea chhola (ছোলা), crispy aloo chop (আলুর চপ), crunchy beguni (বেগুনি), hot piyaju (পিয়াজু)
- Dinner Main Course: Fragrant Basmati/Kalijira Polao (পোলাও), rich chicken roast (রোস্ট), boiled egg, and potato curry.

## 3. Catering & Banquet Capabilities

The resort has proven operational capability in handling large-format catered events:
- **School & College Reunions:** Demonstrated in the 40th anniversary reunion of Dhaka Govt. Muslim High School (Batch 1985) and DUFA 9596 alumni events, serving extensive multi-course buffet luncheons.
- **Wedding Feasts (Biye & Gaye Holud):** Custom wedding catering featuring traditional Kacchi Biryani, Chicken Roast, Borhani, and dessert stations upon contract booking.
