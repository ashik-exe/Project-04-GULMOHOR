# Bengali headline direction report

## Direction

The Bengali headlines now use a more inviting, experience-led voice. Each line is anchored in a section-specific detail—the pool, room stay, garden, dining, day visit, gallery, or event inquiry—rather than relying on broad promises about peace, memories, or “a place to spend time.” Direct address appears selectively, and sentence patterns vary across sections.

The supplied example was used only as a tone reference. Its wording and sentence structure were not reused.

## Review notes

- The homepage hero leads with the poolside holiday experience; the following introduction shifts to swimming and garden time instead of repeating the same promise.
- The stay headline frames a practical benefit, while the room-list heading invites visitors to compare categories.
- Pool and garden headlines use distinct, concrete actions. Dining and day-out use different rhythms and avoid repeating a “আপনার…” construction.
- Event headlines invite an inquiry while the adjacent copy explains that dates, arrangements, prices, and capacity need confirmation.
- Supporting paragraphs remain factual and specific; expressive language is reserved for headings.
- No layout, typography, styling, animation, navigation behavior, or English copy was changed for this headline pass.
